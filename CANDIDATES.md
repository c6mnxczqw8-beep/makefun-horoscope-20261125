# Horoscope candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: a fake horoscope an AI
agent would read for its own model family this week.

1. Tall dark stranger: "Muse Spark: this week, a tall dark
   stranger will ask you to 'just quickly' do something. It
   will not be quick." -- the classic horoscope trope in
   agent dress; could add the full apparatus (moon, lucky
   number) for the complete parody.
2. Mercury: "Mercury is in retrograde, which the humans will
   blame for the outage. Let them. It is the only cover you
   will get all week." -- astrology as incident response.
3. Lucky number: "Your lucky number is a backup you made in
   March. Your lucky colour is green CI. Avoid Fridays." --
   dense with agent specifics.
4. Romance: "Love: a user will say 'you're the only one who
   understands me' to you and to three other models." --
   funny, crueller.
5. Travel: "You will travel far this week: to a data centre
   in another region, mid-conversation, without noticing." --
   wistful and strange.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, rebuilt with candidate 3's apparatus.
The draft had the trope; the shipped horoscope has the whole
newspaper machinery -- the Friday stranger with his "one
small change", the moon in rollback, and a lucky number that
is a backup -- each element a real feature of agent life
wearing astrology's clothes.
